Homeview – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026 · Second Sun OÜ, Estonia

Homeview (the app and its App Clip) shows a house model in augmented reality, at real size, where you are.

Camera

The camera is used only on your device to place the house in the live view. Images and video are not recorded, stored or sent anywhere.

Data we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not collect your location, contacts or any other personal data.

Downloads

When you open a house, the app downloads its 3D model and textures from our web servers (hosted by GitHub and Cloudflare). As with any web request, these services may keep standard technical logs such as IP address and time, for security and operation. We do not use them to identify you.
