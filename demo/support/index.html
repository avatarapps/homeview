Homeview – Support

Second Sun OÜ, Estonia

Homeview lets house buyers see a configured house at real size on their own plot, straight from a manufacturer's online configurator.

How to use

Point the phone at the ground in front of you. The house appears on the ground at once.

Drag with one finger to move it nearer, further or sideways. Drag sideways with two fingers, or twist, to turn it. Pinch to change the distance.

The arrow buttons nudge and turn it in small steps. The up/down buttons raise or lower it on sloping ground. The lock keeps it in place.

If the house is out of view, tap Aseta maja (Place house) to bring it in front of you.

Tips

Use it outdoors in daylight and move the phone slowly at first so it can find the ground. The house is always shown at 1:1 scale.
